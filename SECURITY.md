# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Don't report security vulnerabilities in public issues, pull requests, or discussions.

Report them privately through GitHub: open the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**, or go directly to https://github.com/elektronaut/vector2d/security/advisories/new.

The principles in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) apply here too: describe what you observed, give minimal steps you actually ran, and include raw output. Report what you verified; the maintainers will assess the impact.
